## Key Policies Included

### 🚫 No Cancellation
- Orders cannot be cancelled once confirmed
- Robots are pre-programmed for each order
- No exceptions

### 💰 Full Payment Required
- bKash only
- Payment before delivery
- No COD, no partial payments

### 📦 Delivery Charges
- Customer pays all delivery charges
- Based on location and delivery speed
- Confirmed during order call

### ⏱️ Delivery Time
- Standard: 3-5 days
- Express: 1-2 days
- May vary by location

### ✅ Mandatory Inspection
- Must inspect at delivery
- Check for damage/missing parts
- Video unboxing recommended
- No claims after acceptance

### 🔄 No Returns/Exchanges
- No returns after delivery acceptance
- Replacement only for defects within 48 hours
- Must provide video evidence

### 🛡️ 7-Day Warranty
- Covers manufacturing defects only
- No physical/water damage coverage
- Battery not covered
- User modifications void warranty

### 🔧 30-Day Free Support
- Phone/WhatsApp support
- Operation guidance
- Basic troubleshooting
- Paid after 30 days

### 📝 Order Confirmation
- Call within 2-4 hours
- Must respond within 24 hours
- Auto-cancelled if no response

### ⚖️ Legal Terms
- Bangladesh law applies
- Dhaka courts jurisdiction
- All sales final

## Customer Journey with Terms

1. **Customer browses products** → Sees products
2. **Adds to cart** → Cart functionality works
3. **Clicks checkout** → Opens checkout modal
4. **Fills personal info** → Name, phone, email
5. **Fills address** → Street, city, district
6. **Selects delivery type** → Standard or Express
7. **Reads terms summary** → In checkbox section
8. **Clicks terms link** (optional) → Opens full terms section
9. **Checks acceptance box** → Required to proceed
10. **Submits order** → Validation runs
11. **If terms not accepted** → Error notification
12. **If accepted** → Order submitted successfully
